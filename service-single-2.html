Overview

Business Growth

We support business expansion into the U.S. via strategic visa planning for your key people. Our experts will guide you through the L-1 visa for intracompany transferees or the E-2 investor visa to ensure that your process is smooth. We cover every detail from business plan creation to the final application submission for approval success.

Focus on Target

Our focused approach ensures we pinpoint the perfect visa category for your personal profile and your goals. We review your unique qualifications, potential for investment, and long-term plans to advise the most suitable path, whether for work, study, or for residency.

Monthly Email Report

You can stay fully informed with our detailed monthly case status updates that are sent directly to your email. These reports list every action taken, all upcoming deadlines, and the complete progress of your case, providing total transparency.

Marketing Analysis

We provide an in-depth analysis of your specific case, similar to a market study, to find all of its strengths and any possible challenges. This lets us build a strong application plan, and present your profile in a compelling way to the USCIS.
