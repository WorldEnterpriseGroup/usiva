What documents do I need to start my journey?

We recommend gathering personal identification, financial records, educational documents, and any evidence of professional achievements. Our team will review your case to ensure nothing is overlooked.

How does USIVA simplify the process?

USIVA offers expert consultations, detailed checklists, and comprehensive support to navigate both paperwork and procedural requirements with ease.

What is the typical timeline for a U.S. visa?

Timelines vary based on individual cases and visa types; however, with our proactive approach and expert guidance, many clients experience a smooth and timely process.
