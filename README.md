# USIVA - U.S. Immigration and Visa Association

Static public site for USIVA, the US Council visa-readiness and compliance planning companion to US Fellows.

## Current public pages

- `index.html` introduces USIVA and links to US Fellows.
- `international-rd-scholars.html` explains visa-readiness planning for the US Fellows International R&D Scholars Program.

USIVA content is informational planning content. It does not guarantee admission, employment, funding, visa issuance, or government approval, and it does not replace individualized legal advice.
