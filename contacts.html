The question brief

Four lines are enough to make a handoff useful.

Write these down before you open a message, meeting, or application portal. Leave out identifying numbers until the recipient gives you a verified way to share them.

Current stage
Exploring, preparing, admitted, studying, researching, or planning a next step.
Purpose
Study, research, training, travel, assistantship, internship, or employment question.
Decision date
The deadline, start date, interview, travel date, or authorization window that matters.
Unclear item
The instruction, document, activity, or promise you need the responsible authority to verify.

A deliberate limit

There is no public submission form on this page.

This route does not connect to an intake endpoint, CRM, or case-management workflow. That is why it does not ask for your name, passport, address, or documents.

If a person or provider asks for sensitive information, verify the destination independently and confirm why it is needed before sharing it. For individualized immigration advice, consult a licensed attorney in the relevant jurisdiction.
