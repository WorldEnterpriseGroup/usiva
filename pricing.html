Before any payment

Ask for the terms in writing.

A careful scope note should let you identify who is providing the work, what the reader will receive, what is not included, which dates and sources matter, and where individualized legal questions must go.

  1. 01
    Name the provider.

    Do not infer a relationship from a logo, a referral, or a public planning page.

  2. 02
    Name the deliverable.

    Ask whether the work is education, document organization, a consultation, a filing service, or something else.

  3. 03
    Name the boundary.

    Confirm what the provider cannot decide and which official instruction controls the next action.

  4. 04
    Name the next check.

    Rules, school instructions, sponsor requirements, and fees can change; ask when the information was last verified.

Questions worth carrying

A price is not the same thing as a result.

Use these prompts to keep a commercial conversation separate from an immigration decision.

Does a payment guarantee admission or a visa?

No. USIVA does not promise admission, employment, funding, visa issuance, or government approval. Any provider should state its own scope without implying control over an outside decision.

Can a planning page tell me whether I qualify?

General information can help you identify questions. A personal eligibility analysis belongs with the school, sponsor, government source, or licensed immigration attorney responsible for the matter.

Why is there no number on this page?

There is no published USIVA price list in this public site. A responsible amount, if any, requires a real scope and direct confirmation of the provider and terms.

What should I do if a claim sounds too certain?

Ask for the source, the date checked, the person or institution that controls the decision, and the written terms. Pause before sending money or beginning an activity you do not understand.
