The USIVA desk

The useful immigration question is usually earlier than the form.

These notes are designed to help readers slow down at the right moment: before a document is missing, before an activity is assumed to be authorized, and before a general promise gets mistaken for a personal answer.

EDITOR'S NOTE
01

What belongs here

Planning content with a source trail.

USIVA field notes are not case studies, client testimonials, or predictions. They are short ways into questions about documentation, timing, status, work authorization, school coordination, and the point at which individualized advice becomes the responsible next step.

Read them as orientation. Then verify the current instruction with the school, sponsor, government source, or licensed attorney that controls your situation.

Scope

The source protocol

Read broadly. Decide narrowly.

01Find the primary source. Government pages, school instructions, and sponsor guidance may be more current than a summary.

02Check the date and fit. A rule for one status, program, or location may not answer another.

03Escalate the personal facts. General content cannot resolve a prior refusal, status concern, or individualized legal question.
